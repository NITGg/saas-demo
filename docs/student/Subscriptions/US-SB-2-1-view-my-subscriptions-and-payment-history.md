# **US-SB-2-1: View My Subscriptions and Payment History**

**As a** student,\
**I want to** view my subscriptions and payment history,\
**So that** I can track my course access and previous payments.
===============================================================

## **Flow**

# 1. **🎓 Student** → Opens `My Subscriptions`

2. **⚙️ System** → Displays the active subscription

3. **⚙️ System** → Displays previous subscriptions

4. **🎓 Student** → Opens payment history

5. **⚙️ System** → Displays previous payment transactions

## **Subscription Information**

# - Subscription name

- Activation date

- Expiration date

- Remaining days

- Subscription status

- Available courses

## **Payment Information**

# - Subscription name

- Payment amount

- Payment date

- Payment method

- Payment status

- Transaction number

## **Notes**

# - The student can only view their own subscriptions and payments.

- The active subscription appears first.

- Expired subscriptions remain in history.

- Failed payments do not activate a subscription.

- Payment records remain available after expiration.
